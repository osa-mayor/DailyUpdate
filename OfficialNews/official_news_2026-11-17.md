## 3. [AlphaGenome 2: Deciphering how cells read DNA to better understand disease](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphagenome-2-deciphering-how-cells-read-dna-to-better-understand-disease/)
**Source**: Google DeepMind | **Category**: Science | **Release Type**: official_update

- 유전적 변이가 인간 세포 유형에 미치는 영향을 예측
- 질병에 대한 이해를 높이기 위한 기술적 토대 제공

**태그**: Google DeepMind, AlphaGenome 2, DNA sequence model

### 원문 설명
AlphaGenome 2 is our next-generation DNA sequence model that bridges single-cell biology and human genetics, predicting how variants affect human cell types.
